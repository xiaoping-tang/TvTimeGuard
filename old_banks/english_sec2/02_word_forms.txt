U|The country faced several ___ (crisis) that year.|crises|Nouns ending in -is change to -es: crisis → crises.
U|The ___ (criterion) for selecting the team were announced yesterday.|criteria|The plural of "criterion" is "criteria".
U|Scientists study natural ___ (phenomenon) such as rainbows and lightning.|phenomena|The plural of "phenomenon" is "phenomena".
U|The ___ (thief) were caught by the police the next day.|thieves|Nouns ending in -f often change to -ves: thief → thieves.
U|We spotted several ___ (deer) drinking at the stream.|deer|"Deer" stays the same in the plural.
U|The two ___ (analysis) gave very different results.|analyses|Nouns ending in -is change to -es: analysis → analyses.
U|The desert garden is full of ___ (cactus).|cacti/cactuses|The plural of "cactus" is "cacti" (or "cactuses").
U|Both ___ (hypothesis) were proven wrong by the experiment.|hypotheses|Nouns ending in -is change to -es: hypothesis → hypotheses.
U|The ___ (echo) in the cave frightened the young campers.|echoes|Some nouns ending in -o add -es: echo → echoes.
U|Harmful ___ (bacterium) can multiply quickly in warm food.|bacteria|The plural of "bacterium" is "bacteria".
U|The ___ (chief) of the two tribes met to sign a peace deal.|chiefs|"Chief" simply adds -s, unlike "thief".
U|Cut each of the apples into two ___ (half).|halves|Nouns ending in -f often change to -ves: half → halves.
A|This is the ___ (bad) storm we have had in years.|worst|The superlative of "bad" is "worst".
A|The more you practise, the ___ (good) you will become.|better|"The more..., the better...": good → better.
A|The ___ (early) you start, the sooner you will finish.|earlier|"The + comparative..., the + comparative...": early → earlier.
A|Of the two sisters, Mei is the ___ (tall).|taller|When comparing only two, use the comparative: taller.
A|My grandfather is the ___ (old) member of our family.|oldest/eldest|The superlative of "old" is "oldest" (or "eldest" for family).
A|Please contact the office if you need any ___ (far) information.|further|"Further" means "more" or "additional".
A|The station was ___ (far) away than I had thought.|farther/further|The comparative of "far" for distance is "farther" or "further".
A|Ahmad has ___ (little) money than his brother.|less|The comparative of "little" is "less".
A|Of all the candidates, she made the ___ (few) mistakes.|fewest|The superlative of "few" is "fewest".
A|This is the ___ (simple) solution of all.|simplest|The superlative of "simple" is "simplest".
A|The traffic is getting ___ (bad) and worse every day.|worse|"Worse and worse" shows something getting more and more bad.
A|Have you read the ___ (late) issue of the magazine?|latest|"Latest" means the newest.
A|This suitcase is ___ (heavy) than it looks.|heavier|Change y to i and add -er: heavy → heavier.
A|My aunt is the ___ (happy) person I know.|happiest|Change y to i and add -est: happy → happiest.
A|The ___ (little) you worry, the better you will sleep.|less|"The less..., the better...": little → less.
